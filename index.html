Kafri Artisan Breads

Sourdough · Tenafly, NJ

Kafri Artisan Breads is a small sourdough bakery in Tenafly, NJ. Our sourdough is naturally leavened and slowly fermented, made with organic flour from Janie's Mill. We bake in small batches a few days a week: rustic sourdough loaves and Jerusalem bagels, a seasonal fall collection, and challah and babka for Shabbat. Order online, then pick up fresh from the oven or get local delivery in Bergen County.
